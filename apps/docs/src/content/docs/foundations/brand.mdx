Tanrei (端麗) means clean and well-formed. The name is a brief for the system: interfaces that are quiet, precise and easy to read, on every platform a product ships to. Tanrei is an independent project.

## The marks

Tanrei has two marks, and uses one at a time.

- **The symbol** is the T of Shippori Mincho, with its corners eased. It goes where the name is not read: an app icon, a favicon, the header of this site.
- **The wordmark** is "Tanrei" set in Shippori Mincho Bold. It goes where the name is read: a cover, a page title, the image of a shared link.

The symbol sits a little below the centre of its square frame. This is an optical correction: the T carries its weight at the top, and the lower position makes it look centred.

## Color

Both marks are drawn in one color, `sys.color.on-surface`, on the surface they stand on. Because the color is a token and not part of the drawing, the marks follow the light and dark schemes and every contrast level. Each use takes on-surface: the header of this site paints its symbol with `comp.app-bar.brand.color`, which is on-surface.

## Clear space

<BrandFigure
  title="Clear space"
  panels={[{ mark: 'symbol', height: 160, clearSpace: true, caption: 'A quarter of the ink’s height on every side' }]}
/>

Keep a quarter of the symbol's height clear on every side. Measure it from the ink, not from the frame. Around the wordmark, keep half its x-height clear. Nothing else enters that space: no text, no other mark and no edge of the screen.

## Minimum size

<BrandFigure
  title="Minimum size"
  panels={[
    { mark: 'symbol', height: 'min', caption: 'The symbol: a 24 px frame' },
    { mark: 'wordmark', height: 'min', caption: 'The wordmark: a 24 px capital' },
  ]}
/>

Never draw the symbol smaller than a 24 px frame, and never draw the wordmark with a capital less than 24 px high.

The favicon is the one exception, because a browser draws it at 16 px. It is one SVG. The ICO and PNG files beside it are drawings of the same image at the sizes browsers ask for, not a second design. At 16 px its edges soften, and that is accepted.

### Do

- Use one mark at a time: the symbol where the name is not read, the wordmark where it is.
- Draw the mark in on-surface, on the surface of the scheme it stands in.
- Where a platform needs an opaque icon, such as a home screen or a raster favicon, put the symbol on the light scheme's surface. The square belongs to the platform, not to the mark.
- Keep the symbol where it sits in its frame. Don't move it to the frame's centre.

### Don't

- Don't recolor a mark, and don't add a gradient, an outline, a shadow or a background shape.
- Don't stretch, rotate or crop a mark.
- Don't build a lockup of the symbol and the wordmark, and don't set the name as text beside the symbol.
- Don't set the wordmark again in another typeface. Use its drawing.
- Don't use the characters 端麗 as a mark. They are an ornament of the design file's cover, not part of the identity.

## The character of the system

The brand is also the way the system looks.

### Content first, chrome last

Surfaces are near-monochrome and borders are one-pixel hairlines, so the product's content is the most colorful thing on the screen. The primary action is black on white or white on black. A single blue accent exists, and it is reserved for the one thing that must be found at once: the keyboard focus ring. A link is told by its underline and the chosen item by the primary color, so nothing else on the screen is blue.

### Structure you can see

Hierarchy comes from alignment, spacing and hairlines rather than from heavy shadows or tinted panels. Elevation is a ring plus a very soft shadow.

### Additive expression

The default is restrained. Expressive choices such as heavier emphasis steps, larger and smoother corners and springier motion are extra tokens layered on the same structure, never a fork of it.
