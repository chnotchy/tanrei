A pagination pages one list by number: search results, the posts of a blog, the rows of a table. The page before and the page after in a sequence of titled pages, such as the chapters of a guide, are [Previous and next links](/components/previous-next-links/): a title says where it leads, a page number does not.

### Usage

Give the current page and how many pages there are. The pagination works out which numbers to show. With `href`, every page is a link to its own address. Without it, the pages are buttons and you change what is shown when the page changes.

<Tabs syncKey="platform">
  <TabItem label="Web">
    ```html
    <!-- Links: each page has an address a person can bookmark, share or open in a tab. {page} is filled with its number. -->
    <tanrei-pagination label="Search results pages" page="3" count="12" href="/results/?page={page}"
      previous-label="Previous page" next-label="Next page" page-label="Page {page}"></tanrei-pagination>

    <!-- Buttons: the page changes in place. The element sets its own page, then fires change; cancel it to keep the page. -->
    <tanrei-pagination id="pages" label="Pages" page="1" count="10"
      previous-label="Previous page" next-label="Next page" page-label="Page {page}"></tanrei-pagination>

    <script type="module">
      import '@tanrei/web/components/pagination';
      document.querySelector('#pages').addEventListener('change', (e) => showPage(e.detail.page));
    </script>
    ```
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="React">
    ```tsx
    import { Pagination } from '@tanrei/react';

    <Pagination label="Pages" page={page} count={10}
      previousLabel="Previous page" nextLabel="Next page" pageLabel="Page {page}"
      onChange={(e) => setPage(e.detail.page)} />
    ```
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

SwiftUI, Compose, Flutter and React Native are planned: the names beside each platform at the top of this page are the ones the spec gives them, and nothing ships there yet.

The words are yours: Tanrei ships none. Give them on each element, or once for the whole product with `setWords()` (`previousPageLabel`, `nextPageLabel`, `firstPageLabel`, `lastPageLabel`, `pageLabel`, `pageCountText`).

- **Which numbers are shown.** The first and the last page (`boundaries`, 1 by default), the current page and one page on each side of it (`siblings`, 1 by default). The pages left out between them are one ellipsis. Near an end, more pages are shown on the other side, so the row holds the same number of places on every page and keeps its width.
- **The current page** is drawn filled and is exposed as the current one. Activating it does nothing.
- **Previous and next** are icon-only. They are disabled on the first and on the last page, and keep their places. **First and last** are drawn on either side of them with `first-last`.
- **The short form** (`variant="compact"`) draws previous and next around your sentence, "Page 3 of 10". Without a sentence it draws the two numbers with a slash, "3 / 10", and a development build reports that the words are missing. The numbered form draws the short one by itself where it does not fit the width it is given.
- **Two sizes**: `medium` (40) and `small` (32), for a dense place such as the status line of a [Table](/components/table/).
- **Right to left**, the row is laid from the right and the arrows are mirrored. The numbers are drawn in the digits of the page's language.

## Guidelines

### Do

- Put it after the list it pages, and before the list too where the list is long.
- Use links (`href`) when each page has an address of its own, and buttons when the page changes in place.
- Under a [Table](/components/table/), use the short form at size `small` in the status line, beside a [Select](/components/select/) for the rows per page where people choose it. The range of rows shown ("1–10 of 248") is the table's status text.
- When the page changes, say what changed where the content changes: a heading, or the table's status text. The pagination announces nothing.

### Don't

- Don't use it for the page before and after in a sequence of titled pages. That is [Previous and next links](/components/previous-next-links/).
- Don't show it for a list of one page.
- Don't hide previous and next on the first and last page. They are disabled and keep their places.

## Accessibility

<ComponentAccessibility name="pagination" />

### What the component handles

- One navigation landmark around one list. Each page and each enabled control is a link or a button and a stop in the focus order.
- The current page is exposed with `aria-current="page"`, and drawn filled, not only in another color.
- Disabled controls are out of the focus order. The arrows and the ellipsis are hidden from assistive technology.
- A page's name holds the number a person sees, so a person who says what they see can activate it.

### What you need to do

- Name the landmark with `label`, in your product's language. A page has other navigation, and the name tells this one apart.
- Give the controls their names (`previous-label`, `next-label`, and `first-label` and `last-label` with `first-last`) and a page its name with `page-label`. Tanrei ships no words of its own.
- Say what changed when you replace the list. The pagination keeps focus on one of its items: the control that was activated, or the page now current where that control is disabled.
