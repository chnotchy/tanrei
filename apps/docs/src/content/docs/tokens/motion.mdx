## Durations by role

How long a change takes, by what moves: a change of state, a selection, a box that resizes, content that opens in place, and each kind of surface that appears and leaves. A component's duration token aliases its role, so a layer re-times every component of one role in one line. A surface leaves faster than it appears. The roles follow the motion dial.

## Easing

The curves, and the four roles a component eases a change by: `enter` for a surface that appears, `exit` for one that leaves, `state` for a change of state in place, `spatial` for something that moves or resizes in place. Each role names a curve of the scale.

<MotionTokens part="easing" />

## Where a surface appears from

Every surface that appears — a menu, a list, a popover, a tooltip, a dialog, a snackbar — starts from the same opacity, scale, distance and blur, and leaves the same way back. One choice of kind sets them together; a layer chooses another, or pins one of the values.

## Springs

Each spring has four sub-tokens. `stiffness` and `damping` drive platforms with spring animation; `duration` and `easing` are the approximation used where springs are unavailable, such as CSS.
