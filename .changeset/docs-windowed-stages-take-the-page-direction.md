Documentation site: a fixture drawn in a window of its own on a component page's Specs tab is drawn in the page's
direction when it names none, as the stages drawn in the page are. A window is a document of its own, which inherits
nothing from the page, and its root took a direction only from the frame's action: every window of an Arabic page
(the app shell's and the navigation drawer's) was drawn left to right. A fixture that names a direction keeps it, and
the frame's direction action still turns its window.
