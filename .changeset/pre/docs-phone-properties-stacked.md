On a phone, or wherever a component page's Specs column is narrow, each row of a table of sentences (the properties,
anatomy, slots, events and behaviour tables) now stands as one block: the name on its line, a property's type and
default on the next after their columns' names, and the description under them at the column's full width. Before, a
property's row was as tall as its description at its least width, its name, type and default sat in an empty band, and
the description scrolled off the side. A wide column keeps the tables as they were, and the tables are still tables to
assistive technology, each cell read with its column's header.
