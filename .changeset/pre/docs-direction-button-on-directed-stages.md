Documentation site: a Specs tab stage whose fixture names a direction (Select's `open-rtl`, App shell's
`expanded-rtl`, …) no longer offers the button that turns the stage to the other direction. The fixture's stage
carries its own direction, over the frame's, so the button turned nothing; a fixture that names a direction exists to
show it. A stage whose fixture names none still offers it, in the page and in a window of its own.
