The cloak (`@tanrei/web/cloak.css`) holds a button that is defined before the connected button group it stands in at
the room the group will give it: at least the target along the join (the touch target under a coarse pointer), and in
a row the connected outer padding at its ends. Each element's module may arrive on its own; until the group's did, a
column of icon-only buttons drew at their own height and grew to the touch target when the group was defined, and what
was below them moved. A button that names no size in a group that names one is still drawn at its own size until the
group is defined: the group passes its size by script.
